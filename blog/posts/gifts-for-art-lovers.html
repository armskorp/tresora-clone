Shopping for someone who stops at every painting in a museum is harder than it looks. Prints feel impersonal, art books sit on a shelf, and museum-shop magnets rarely say "I thought about you." The best gifts for art lovers bring a painting they love into everyday life: at the table, with a cup of tea, with a glass of wine on a quiet evening.

Here is how to choose one, and six ideas inspired by the artists people love most.

How to choose a gift for an art lover

  • Start with the artist, not the object. Notice the painting on her phone background, the poster in her hallway, the museum she keeps talking about.
  • Pick something she will use. A cup, a glass, a candle or a throw gets touched every day. A framed print alone gets noticed once.
  • Make the unboxing part of the gift. A box that arrives ready to give feels like a small event.
