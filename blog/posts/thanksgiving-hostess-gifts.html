What makes a good Thanksgiving hostess gift

  • It asks nothing of her on the day. No arranging, no chilling, no finding a spot for it right now. A closed, beautifully wrapped box can wait on the console until the guests go home.
  • It is for her, not for the party. The best hostess gifts are something she will enjoy on a quiet evening after the holidays, not something she feels she must open and serve.
  • It fits a person who loves hosting. Candles, a table runner, a cheese board, good glasses: things that make the next gathering even easier.
  • It looks generous without being too much. Between $50 and $70 is the sweet spot for a thoughtful hostess gift from a guest or a couple.

Hostess gift etiquette, in three lines

  • Hand it over at the door and say it is for later. That one sentence takes all the pressure off her.
  • Do not expect it to be opened at the table. A hostess gift is a thank-you, not a contribution to the meal.
  • Bring something for the meal separately if you offered. The pie you promised is not the hostess gift.
